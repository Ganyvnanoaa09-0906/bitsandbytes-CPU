record(igpu): close the iGPU line, including the cause I could not find

Positive: the iGPU is usable on its own terms -- 300 GFLOPS on non-cubic shapes, correct,
where CLBlast cannot run its own sample shape on this machine at all, and 28.9 GB/s
streaming against the CPU's 13.2, i.e. its memory efficiency is 2.2x the CPU's. That last
figure had not been noticed before; what is constrained is the CPU's memory subsystem, not
the machine's memory.

Negative, and together these close every way of using it beside the CPU:

  splitting one matmul between the devices                 0.68-0.91x
  two independent workloads concurrently                   0.81x
  Muon's Newton-Schulz offloaded, synchronous alternation  0.23x of ideal
  the same, fully device-resident and correct              0.39x of ideal
  batched enqueue                                          0.92x, but it did one product
                                                           per matrix rather than the
                                                           full five-iteration NS

Attribution gives the mechanism: under concurrency the CPU side barely moves (0.88x) while
the iGPU halves (1.84x); combined streaming bandwidth is 8.0 GB/s, below the iGPU's own
28.9; and even a compute-only test with no memory traffic interferes by 1.7x. The root
cause is that an APU has no dedicated video memory, so the iGPU shares one DDR4-2667 dual
channel (23.7 GB/s measured pure read) with the CPU.

And its own 300 GFLOPS ceiling is unexplained. Pure compute reaches 1089 GFLOPS, 95% of
peak, so the 3.6x gap is data supply; CLBlast reaches 493.8 on this machine, so 300 is not
a hardware limit. Seven variants were slower, and two explanations I offered were both
contradicted -- A's reads are a broadcast rather than a conflict, and the real inner loop
already exceeds what reads alone can sustain. The measurement I never made is the
throughput of local memory itself; measure_lds_throughput.py is written and queued, with
three access patterns. Until that number exists this does not claim a cause. Five confident
mechanisms without measurements behind them were overturned today, two of them here.

Also added: measure_guard.py, which refuses to benchmark while another substantial job is
running and stamps every result with its conditions. It exists because three measurements
were taken while the Muon A/B held 4.79 of 6 cores, all of them suspect as a result. The
user found that one.
